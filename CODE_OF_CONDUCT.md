# Code of conduct

## Scope and expectations

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces. Treat participants respectfully, welcome questions, give constructive feedback and discuss the work on its merits. Respect privacy, differing experience levels and boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, publishing private information without consent, and persistent personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Reporting

Send conduct concerns privately to richardnoragon@mac.com. Include relevant links, dates, a description of the behavior and any immediate needs. Share only the personal information needed to investigate. Do not post confidential reports in public issues.

The maintainer will review reports, seek clarification when needed and limit disclosure to what is necessary to address the concern. No response-time guarantee is established. If a report concerns the maintainer, an independent escalation contact has not yet been appointed.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and repetition, the maintainer may request a correction, remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily or remove a participant from project spaces. Serious threats or privacy violations may result in immediate restriction. Decisions should state the reason and scope to the affected participant where practicable. Requests for reconsideration can be sent to the same private address with relevant context.
